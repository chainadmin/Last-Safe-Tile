GAME CONCEPT
“LAST SAFE TILE”

Genre: Reflex / Survival
Playtime per run: 10–45 seconds
Controls: One finger / tap
Platform: Web → Play Store / App Store later
Engine: Same stack you’re already using (Phaser / JS)

🧠 CORE IDEA (Simple, Addictive)

You’re standing on a grid of tiles.

Every second, one tile becomes unsafe

Unsafe tiles crack, then fall away

You must tap to move to a safe tile before yours disappears

The grid shrinks over time

Survive as long as possible

The tension:

You always feel like:

“I can survive one more move…”

🌟 UNIQUE TWIST (this is what makes it sell)
You don’t choose where to go — you choose WHEN to move

You tap once to move

Your character auto-moves to the safest available tile

The longer you wait before tapping:

the riskier the move becomes

but the higher your score multiplier

So players constantly choose:

Move early (safe, low score)

Wait longer (dangerous, big score)

💥 This creates natural monetization tension without being annoying.

🧩 GAME RULES (Exact)
Board

Starts as 5×5

Tiles have 3 states:

✅ Safe

⚠️ Cracking (0.7s warning)

❌ Gone

Each turn

A random safe tile becomes ⚠️ cracking

After 0.7s → tile disappears

Player must tap before their tile disappears

Difficulty scaling

After every 5 seconds:

Crack speed increases

Board shrinks by removing outer tiles

After 20 seconds:

Two tiles crack at once

After 30 seconds:

Fake “safe” tiles appear (visual only)

🧮 SCORING

Base score: 1 point per second

Risk multiplier:

Move immediately → ×1

Wait until last 30% of timer → up to ×3

Final score = time × multiplier

This makes “almost died” moments rewarding.

💀 FAIL CONDITION

Your tile disappears while you’re still on it

Instant, clear, fair.

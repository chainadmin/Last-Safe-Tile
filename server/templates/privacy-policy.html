Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2, 2026

Chain Software Group ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Last Safe Tile mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Last Safe Tile is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

  • Game Data: Your game progress, high scores, coins, and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.
  • Purchase Information: When you make in-app purchases, payment processing is handled by Apple (App Store), Google (Play Store), or our payment processor Authorize.net. We do not store your payment card details.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic device information (device type, operating system) for app compatibility and crash reporting purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Prevent fraud and ensure transaction security

Data Storage

Last Safe Tile uses a local-first approach. Your game data (scores, coins, settings) is stored directly on your device using local storage. This means:

  • Your data stays on your device
  • We do not have access to your game progress
  • Uninstalling the app will remove your local data

Third-Party Services

Our App may use the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store: For app distribution and in-app purchases
  • Authorize.net: For payment processing on web platforms

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Children's Privacy

Last Safe Tile does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game is designed for general audiences and does not require account creation or personal data submission.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction of data. Payment transactions are processed through secure, PCI-compliant payment processors.

Your Rights

Since game data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can:

  • Clear app data through your device settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
